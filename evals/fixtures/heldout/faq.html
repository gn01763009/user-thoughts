FAQ

Questions and answers

Booking, your day with us and membership.

Booking

How far ahead can I book?

Up to 30 days ahead, for any of the four spaces.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Cancel free of charge until 18:00 the day before, from My bookings. After that the day cannot be refunded, because we have held the desk for you.

Can I book for someone else?

Yes. Put their name on the booking so reception knows who to expect.

On the day

Why do you ask for a photo ID?

Reception checks a photo ID on your first visit only, so our hosts know who is in the building. After that your booking reference is enough.

What is included?

A desk for the day, fast Wi-Fi, tea and coffee, and up to 20 printed pages.

When are the spaces open?

Each space keeps its own hours. Sign in and open Spaces to see the address and hours of each one.

Can I bring a guest?

Members have guest days each month. With a day pass, book a second pass in your guest’s name.

Membership

How is membership different from a day pass?

A day pass is one day at one space. Membership gives you a set number of days each month, or a desk every weekday, with a lower price per day.

How do I end a membership?

Email us with 30 days’ notice. There is no minimum term.

Contact

Email hello@pellwick.example and we will reply within one working day. On the day, ask the host at reception.

Privacy

We keep your name, email and bookings to run your account, and we never sell them. Ask us to remove them at any time.

Terms

Desks are for work during opening hours. Please keep calls to the phone booths and leave your desk as you found it.
