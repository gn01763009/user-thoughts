How can we help?

Quick answers below. Still stuck? Write to us and a person replies within one working day.

How do I create my first invoice?

Open the demo or sign in, choose New invoice, pick a client and add your line items. Press Send when you are ready.

How do payment reminders work?

On Pro and Studio, we email your client three days before the due date, on the due date, and a week after. You can change the schedule.

Which currencies can I invoice in?

US dollars, euros, pounds, Canadian dollars and Australian dollars today. Each client keeps their own default.

How do I add a client tax ID?

Open the client, choose Billing details and enter the tax or VAT number. It appears on every invoice to that client.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Account, then Export, gives you a spreadsheet of clients and invoices at any time.
