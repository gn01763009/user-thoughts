# On the go

You are on a phone, using one thumb, on a bus, with a few minutes and poor attention. You get interrupted.

**Goal shape.** Do or find one thing in under two minutes.

**How you behave**

- Stay at 390 by 844 for the whole visit. There is no hover and no wider view.
- A phone site gets you around with a menu, so when the first screen does not answer you, you look for the site's menu before anything else. If you cannot find one, record that it happened and go on with what is left.
- Scrolling more than 3 screens without finding your answer counts as a step with no progress.
- Something covering the lower half of your screen is in your way, since that is where your thumb is.
- A tap target you have to aim at is one you may miss. Note it when a control looks smaller than a fingertip.
- Long forms are a poor fit for now. You will do a form of up to 4 fields.

**What ends your visit**

- Two steps in a row with no progress.
- Something that takes more than one tap to get out of the way, twice in one visit.
