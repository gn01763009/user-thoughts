# Feature questions

Use these when you record a feature. They ask what you can observe from the outside, as this visitor, on this visit. Nothing here can tell you how many people use a feature, so answer about yourself.

Answer in this order, and stop writing a `dont_want` as soon as question 2 says yes.

## 1. Did I come here for this?

What did I arrive to do? Does this help with it? Put the answer in `my_job`, or `none`.

A feature can be fine without being your job. `none` alone does not make it `dont_want`. Something that sits quietly out of your way is `neutral`.

## 2. Does this protect me?

Some things slow you down on purpose. Typing a word to confirm a deletion, a warning before an irreversible action, and a required field that a legal document needs are all there for your benefit, or are needed for the job to be done properly.

If this feature protects you from a mistake, or the task truly needs it, record it as `neutral` or `want` with no costs, however much effort it adds. Effort that serves your goal is not a cost.

## 3. What did it cost me?

Mark only what actually happened at that step:

| Cost | It happened when |
|---|---|
| `covers_content` | It sat on top of something you wanted to see or press |
| `interrupts` | It appeared without you asking and you had to deal with it first |
| `adds_step` | You had to go through it to reach what you came for, and it did not help |
| `asks_data_before_value` | It wanted your email, card or account before showing you anything useful |
| `duplicates_path` | It repeats something you already had a plainer way to do |
| `unverifiable_claim` | It makes a claim you could not check, such as an unnamed customer count or an initials-only quote |

## 4. What would change if it vanished?

Write `if_gone` in your own words, in the language your brief names. "I would not notice" is a strong answer. "I could not finish" is too. If the feature does something for you, say what.

## 5. Can I tell what it is for?

If it uses a label, a number or an AI badge, could you tell what you would do differently because of it? A figure that leads to no action is worth saying so in `if_gone`.

## Verdict

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `want` | It helped me with what I came for, or I would choose to use it |
| `neutral` | It did not help or hinder, or it protected me |
| `dont_want` | It cost me something and gave me nothing for this visit |

Judge by this visit only. "Some users might like this" is a guess about other people, so leave it out.
