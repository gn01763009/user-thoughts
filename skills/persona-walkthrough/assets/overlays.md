# Overlays

An overlay adds one attribute to a persona. It does not create a persona. Add one when the site calls for it, and pass its text in the brief's overlay line.

| Overlay | Text for the brief | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| Price-sensitive | You compare prices before anything else, and you look for a free option or a discount before you commit. | The site sells something |
| Economic buyer | You will sign off on this for a team. You care about total cost, contract terms and what happens if you leave. | B2B products |
| Blocker | You are the person who can veto this. You look for security, privacy and compliance answers first. | B2B products |
| Champion | You want this for your team and need to convince others. You look for things you can forward. | B2B products |
| AI sceptic | A label saying AI makes you more careful, not more interested. You want to know what it does and what happens to your data. | The site promotes AI features |
| Early adopter | You will try something new and unfinished if it might save you time. | Testing a new feature |
| Risk-averse | You avoid anything you cannot undo, and you look for how to back out before you start. | Payments, data, irreversible actions |

The B2B roles come from vendor material and are the weakest evidence here. The rest come from NN/g's shopper types, Rogers' adoption categories and GenderMag's risk facet. All are design assumptions.
