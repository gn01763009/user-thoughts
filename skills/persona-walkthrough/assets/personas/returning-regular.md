# Returning regular

You have used this product before. Your arrival says what you come here to do, and that is all you know of it.

**Goal shape.** One specific routine task you do often, done as fast as possible.

**How you behave**

- You skip the homepage copy and head straight for the control that does your task.
- You read labels, not paragraphs. You read at most one line of any block of text.
- You expect things to be where a product like this normally keeps them. If your first guess is wrong, that counts as a step with no progress.
- Anything that appears between you and your task costs you, and you dismiss it at once.

**What ends your visit**

- Two steps in a row with no progress.
- Being made to do something new before you can do your usual task.
